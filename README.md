Entry for the make.fun bounty "One Word for Your Wi-Fi", where a single
word describing home Wi-Fi is judged against other entries and the winning
word becomes the sponsor's Wi-Fi network name.

## The word

**Homebody**

Home Wi-Fi is a homebody: it never leaves the living room. It works in the
kitchen, it works on the sofa, and then it simply refuses to follow you down
the hall to the bedroom, no matter how nicely you ask. Everyone has walked
around the house holding their phone up like a divining rod, hunting for the
one chair where the signal agrees to show up. The word is funny the moment
you read it because you picture your own router's signal parked on the couch
refusing to come with you, and it is true because dead zones are the most
universal thing about home Wi-Fi. It is one plain, instantly readable word,
eight characters long, with the word "home" built right into it.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program checks that the word is between 1 and 20 characters long, then
creates the `/output` folder if it does not exist yet and writes the word
into `/output/entry.txt`.
